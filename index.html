Our Story

Four generations, one family business.

In pursuit of the American dream, John and Doris Crump started a Skelly gas station in Gillette, WY in 1963. A year later, they recognized a need for tires and moved operations to the storefront that still stands as the flagship Big Horn Tire location today.

During the coal boom of the '80s, their son John Jr. Crump saw an opportunity to serve the mining industry's OTR needs and joined the family business, scaling it quickly — later joined by Cathy Crump (Stalcup). Today the Crump family has expanded Big Horn Tire into six locations across three states, remaining a multi-generational, family-owned company built on safety, integrity, quality, and service.
